# Double-Entry General Ledger Engine & Standard Account Rules

## Core Principles
1. **Mathematical Equilibrium**: Every transaction is recorded as equal debits and credits.
2. **Normal Balance Classifications**:
   - Assets: Normal Debit ($+$ on Debit, $-$ on Credit)
   - Expenses / COGS: Normal Debit ($+$ on Debit, $-$ on Credit)
   - Liabilities: Normal Credit ($+$ on Credit, $-$ on Debit)
   - Equity: Normal Credit ($+$ on Credit, $-$ on Debit)
   - Revenue: Normal Credit ($+$ on Credit, $-$ on Debit)

## Journal Posting Workflow
1. Entry created in `DRAFT` status or auto-generated by domain services.
2. Validation ensures `total_debit == total_credit` and `total_debit > 0`.
3. Validation ensures the entry date does not fall within a `CLOSED` financial period.
4. On posting, lines are stamped with timestamp and user ID, and become immutable.
5. In case of corrections, a reversing `VOID` entry is generated.

# Financial Statements & Reporting Engine

## 1. Profit & Loss Statement (P&L / Income Statement)
$$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{COGS}$$
$$\text{Net Operating Income} = \text{Gross Profit} - \text{Operating Expenses}$$

- **Net Revenue**: Gross Sales + Delivery Fees - Discounts
- **COGS**: Food Ingredients + Beverage Stock + Kitchen Wastage
- **Operating Expenses**: Payroll + Rent + Utilities + Maintenance + Marketing + Supplies + Merchant Fees

## 2. Statement of Financial Position (Balance Sheet)
$$\text{Total Assets} = \text{Total Liabilities} + \text{Total Equity}$$

- **Assets**: Cash, Bank, Clearing, Receivables, Inventory
- **Liabilities**: Supplier Payables, Tax Payable
- **Equity**: Capital + Retained Period Income

## 3. General Ledger Trial Balance
$$\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$$
Integrity check validating that General Ledger accounts are in arithmetic equilibrium across all historical entries.
